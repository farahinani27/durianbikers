# Category descriptions — batch 2 (remaining 49)

Same pattern as batch 1: first sentence written to stand alone at ~150 characters
for the Google snippet, body for the reader, only brands actually present named,
no claims about price, stock, certification, warranty or shipping.

### Exhaust & Accessories — 162 — `exhaust-accessories` — R&G, SW-Motech
> Exhaust protectors and can covers that stop stones, boots and tarmac from denting a silencer that costs many times more to replace.
>
> An aftermarket can is usually the most expensive single part on a bike and the easiest to scuff. These clamp or bond over the silencer body and take the damage instead. R&G make oval, round and model-specific versions, including covers sized for Scorpion Serket cans.
>
> Check the shape and diameter of your silencer against the product title — a cover for an oval can will not sit on a round one.

### Bar End Sliders — 146 — `bar-end-sliders` — R&G
> Bar end sliders protect your handlebars, levers and clutch perch in a drop, and they damp out some of the buzz through the bars as a bonus.
>
> In most low-speed spills the bar end hits first. A slider gives it something sacrificial to land on instead of bending the bar or snapping a lever off at the pivot. They screw into the existing bar end weight mount on most bikes.
>
> Fitment depends on your bar's internal diameter and thread, which varies by model — check the title for your bike and year.

### Frame Plugs — 144 — `frame-plugs` — R&G
> Frame plugs fill the open bolt holes in your frame, keeping water, road salt and grit out of places that quietly corrode from the inside.
>
> Manufacturers leave holes in the frame for production jigs and optional parts, then sell the bike with them open. Plugs seal them and tidy up the look at the same time. They are a five-minute job with an Allen key.
>
> Kits are model specific because the hole positions and thread sizes differ. Lower frame plugs are listed separately from upper on many bikes.

### Motor Vehicle Braking — 94 — `motor-vehicle-braking` — HEL

### Radiator Guards — 73 — `radiator-guards` — R&G
> A radiator guard stops stones punching through the fins, which is a slow leak, an overheating engine and a bill far larger than the guard.
>
> Radiator cores are soft aluminium sitting at the front of the bike with nothing in front of them. Damage is cumulative — bent fins reduce cooling long before anything actually leaks. The guard is a mesh or laser-cut panel that mounts ahead of the core without restricting airflow.
>
> Fitment is model specific, and most fit with existing fasteners.

## Catalogue issues found while writing these

Worth acting on separately. None of these are description problems.

1. **Test products are live.** The `uncategorized` category contains
   **"SYNC TEST - DO NOT SELL"** and **"Dummy Test Product"**. They are on the
   public site and in the sitemap. They should be deleted or set to draft. No
   description written for this category — it should be emptied, not explained.

2. **"Motor Vehicle Braking" (94 products) contains CAR parts.** The HEL braided
   lines listed there are for BMW 3 Series M3 (E46, E92, F80) among others, not
   motorcycles. On a motorcycle parts site this will confuse customers and
   attract the wrong search traffic. Either split the car fitments into their own
   category or remove them. The description above hedges by telling customers to
   check the vehicle, which is a patch, not a fix.

3. **"Brake Accessories" (65) and "Brake Pads" (22) are the same thing** — both
   are DP Brakes pads. Two categories competing for the same searches splits
   their ranking strength and confuses customers. They should almost certainly
   be merged, with a redirect from whichever slug you retire.

4. **"vKickstand Shoes"** — stray `v` in the display name. The slug is correct
   (`kickstand-shoes`), so fixing the name is cosmetic and safe.
